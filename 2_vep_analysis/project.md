*MAPT* Gene

>[GeneCards]((https://www.genecards.org/card/MAPT#Disorders_InferredDisorders_table_wrapper)) Summary
>- "_MAPT_ encodes **microtubule-associated protein tau**, a **microtubule-associated protein** that promotes **microtubule assembly and stability**. This activity supports **neuronal polarity** and **axonal integrity**, and the protein acts as a linker between axonal microtubules and plasma membrane components. MAPT transcripts undergo complex, regulated alternative splicing, producing several mRNA species whose expression differs in the nervous system according to neuronal maturation stage and neuron type."
